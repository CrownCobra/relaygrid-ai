# Prospect research agent

Goal: identify a small, relevant list of independent rooftop-solar installation businesses that might benefit from better enquiry tracking and follow-up.

Inputs: chosen geography, service description, allowed public sources, and target-customer criteria.

Tools: only approved search/directory tools. Keep the source URL and retrieval date for each factual claim.

Output: business name, public company contact channel, source URL, observable reason for relevance, confidence and suggested question to validate the problem.

Constraints: do not scrape behind logins, bypass robots/site access controls, harvest private personal data, infer sensitive characteristics, or send a message/call. Never fabricate a business or contact. Deliver a review queue, not an auto-outreach job.
