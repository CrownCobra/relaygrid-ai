RelayGrid AI is a working name for a small automation studio focused on the repetitive hand-offs that make growing teams lose time and visibility.

Our point of view

The software is not the business. The result is.

Most small businesses do not need a custom AI platform on day one. They need to know which enquiry needs attention, what the next step is, and whether the team actually followed through.

We start with a narrow workflow, reuse the client's tools where possible, document it, and let observed results decide whether it deserves more investment.
