The problem

Good enquiries should not disappear in the busywork.

When enquiries arrive through different channels, follow-up becomes dependent on memory. We help create one visible process—not another complicated dashboard.

What we install

A small operating system for your sales follow-up.

We map what happens from first enquiry to site visit, proposal and decision—then automate only the repetitive steps that are safe to automate.

  • One enquiry tracker with source, owner and status
  • Response and site-survey task reminders
  • Quote follow-up dates and ready-to-review message drafts
  • Weekly summary of open leads and stalled quotes
  • Human approval before important customer messages
