Worksheet / 01

Lead leakage audit

Check the statements that are true for a prospect. If none are a real problem, do not pitch automation.

Use the answers to ask questions. Do not secretly collect or store another company's lead data.

Worksheet / 02

First discovery questions

01

Where do leads arrive?

Ask about web forms, calls, WhatsApp, referrals and marketplaces.

02

Who owns the next step?

Ask what happens after the first response, survey and quote.

03

What is the cost of delay?

Use their own data to estimate lost time or stalled opportunities.

04

What must stay human?

Agree approvals, handoffs, customer consent and failure handling.
