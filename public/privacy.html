Privacy notice template

Data should be handled with care.

This notice describes the behaviour of this starter site as delivered. Netlify hosts this site and handles contact submissions when Forms is enabled. Complete the operator and retention details before collecting real enquiries.

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · Draft template; replace with your actual legal information before launch.

1. Who operates this website?

This website is a starter template for a proposed automation studio using the working name RelayGrid AI. The operator's legal name and contact details must be added before public launch. The brand and URL may change.

2. Information submitted through the contact form

The contact workflow sends details to a Netlify Function for validation and rules-based drafting, then requests storage in Netlify Forms. Storage is unverified until the operator sees a matching submission in the Forms inbox. The requested record contains the name, company, email, optional phone, enquiry-volume range, workflow description, rules-based classification, draft response, follow-up status, preferred hand-off, consent flag, generated reference and Netlify submission time for manual review. No AI model receives the enquiry in the no-key setup. The generated reply is a draft and is never sent automatically. Do not submit sensitive or confidential information. Before collecting real enquiries, identify the operator and set a retention/deletion period.

3. Website analytics and cookies

This starter does not intentionally install advertising cookies or a tracking pixel. The optional web font is requested from Google Fonts. Netlify hosts the site and stores submitted form records. The draft is created by simple rules in the no-key setup. Before launch, review the hosting provider terms and disclose any services that are enabled.

4. Future integrations

This workflow has no AI provider, Cloudflare service, webhook, or automated messaging integration. FAQ chat uses preset replies locally in the page; chat text is not submitted or persisted. Contact details and drafts stay visible in the current page after a storage request and disappear when the page is closed or reloaded. Before launch, list every active integration (including hosting, AI inference, webhook, email, voice and messaging), what data it receives, its purpose, retention/deletion process, access controls and the operator contact for privacy requests. Disable any integration whose data handling has not been reviewed.

5. Client data

For any paid workflow, define the client's role as data owner, limit access to what is required, avoid moving production customer data into personal accounts, and agree retention and deletion before implementation. Do not use real customer information to test a new workflow; use synthetic data first.

6. Contact

Before launch, replace this placeholder with a monitored business contact and the operator's legally appropriate information. This is a practical draft, not legal advice. Obtain local advice for applicable privacy and consumer rules.
