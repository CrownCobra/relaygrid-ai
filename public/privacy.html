Privacy notice template

Data should be handled with care.

This notice describes the behaviour of this starter site as delivered. Update it before adding analytics, a CRM, hosted forms or other services.

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · Draft template; replace with your actual legal information before launch.

1. Who operates this website?

This website is a starter template for a proposed automation studio using the working name RelayGrid AI. The operator's legal name and contact details must be added before public launch. The brand and URL may change.

2. Information submitted through the contact form

When secure lead intake is configured, the contact form may store the name, company, email, optional phone, enquiry-volume range, stated workflow problem, consent flag and submission time in the project database so the operator can respond. Server-side intake is protected by anti-spam verification and request limits. If it is not configured or is unavailable, the form prepares an email or WhatsApp draft for the visitor to review and send. Do not submit sensitive or confidential information. Update this paragraph with the real operator, retention period, processor details and deletion contact before collecting real enquiries.

3. Website analytics and cookies

This starter does not intentionally install advertising cookies or a tracking pixel. The optional web font is requested from Google Fonts. If AI chat is enabled, messages are sent to the configured AI inference provider for a response and are not written to this project's database by the chat endpoint. Hosting and AI providers may process operational data under their own terms; review current provider terms and disclose them accurately before launch.

4. Future integrations

If an optional lead webhook is configured, the submitted enquiry may also be sent to that endpoint for task creation or notification. Before launch, list every active integration (including hosting, AI inference, webhook, email, voice and messaging), what data it receives, its purpose, retention/deletion process, access controls and the operator contact for privacy requests. Disable any integration whose data handling has not been reviewed.

5. Client data

For any paid workflow, define the client's role as data owner, limit access to what is required, avoid moving production customer data into personal accounts, and agree retention and deletion before implementation. Do not use real customer information to test a new workflow; use synthetic data first.

6. Contact

Before launch, replace this placeholder with a monitored business contact and the operator's legally appropriate information. This is a practical draft, not legal advice. Obtain local advice for applicable privacy and consumer rules.
