A measurable pilot

Ship a small fix. Measure what changed.

A tightly scoped 14-day pilot is enough to test whether follow-up is a real problem and whether the team will use the solution. We do not promise a specific increase in sales.

01

Discovery

Talk through lead sources, stages, current tools, staff roles and the points where enquiries stall.

02

Baseline

Agree what can be measured: first-response time, site visits scheduled, quote follow-ups due, and recorded outcomes.

03

Implement

Build one thin workflow using client-owned accounts. Test with synthetic data first and add human approval.

04

Review

Review actual use, errors, response times and next-step completion. Keep, change or stop based on evidence.

Pilot scorecard

Measure operations before claiming revenue.

Use the same definitions before and after the pilot. Keep the baseline window and any unusual sales-season effects visible.

  • Median time from enquiry to first human response
  • Share of new leads with an owner and a next step
  • Site surveys booked and attended
  • Quotes due for follow-up, and follow-ups completed
  • Lead outcomes recorded as won, lost or still open
