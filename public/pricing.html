Pricing is a starting hypothesis to test, not a guaranteed offer or income forecast. It assumes simple tools already available to the client. Hosting, API, LLM, WhatsApp, SMS, telephony, email-platform or other paid costs are excluded unless stated in writing. Never start paid usage without the client's explicit approval and funding.

Pricing rules

Protect the margin and the relationship.

Charge for scoped implementation and ongoing support—not for access to free software. Keep the client's accounts and data under their control.

  • Write down what is included and excluded
  • Define what counts as a change request
  • Set an end date and review point for every pilot
  • Use client-owned API keys and accounts when practical
  • Never guarantee leads, sales or energy savings
  • Have an adult/guardian review contracts and payment arrangements if you are under 18

Start without buying tools

Validate the need before building a platform.

Use the discovery calls to confirm the workflow, urgency and actual willingness to pay. The service should finance the next stage—not the other way around.
