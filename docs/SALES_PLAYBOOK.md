# Agent-assisted sales playbook

## Offer

Start with one promise you can deliver: improve lead ownership and follow-up for independent rooftop-solar installers. Do not sell an imaginary all-purpose autonomous company.

## Inbound sequence

1. Visitor opens the website chat or contact page.
2. Assistant answers only from approved service details and labels itself as AI.
3. Assistant asks about workflow goals, not sensitive customer data.
4. Interested buyer submits the form and expressly consents to a response.
5. Lead enters D1; optionally a signed webhook creates a task in Activepieces.
6. The system drafts a personalized, truthful reply. Sending is enabled only after a mail provider, approved message templates and opt-out controls are configured.
7. Human review is required for custom prices, contract terms, client-data access, ROI claims and exceptions.

## Permitted prospect research

Find a small set of relevant businesses through public directories and websites, record source URLs and observable facts, and generate a research summary. Do not scrape behind authentication, evade site restrictions, harvest personal contact information at scale, or send bulk unsolicited messages. An agent should not automatically call a prospect just because a public phone number exists.

## Message principles

- State who the agency is and why the message is relevant.
- No fake urgency, fake scarcity, fake customer logos, fake case studies or guaranteed ROI.
- Give a simple opt-out and respect it across systems.
- Keep follow-up frequency modest and stop when the recipient declines or does not want further contact.
- Do not continue outreach to a suppressed contact because an agent or workflow lost state.

## Pilot success criteria

Measure agreed operational KPIs: time to first response, percent of leads with a named owner/next action, overdue follow-ups, site visits booked and quote follow-up completion. Revenue uplift is measured only where the customer provides reliable data and the analysis accounts for confounders.

RelayGrid AI team
